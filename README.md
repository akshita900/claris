# Claris

> Explainable multimodal ICU alarm playback and contextual reasoning system.

## Overview

Claris is a biomedical AI research project that investigates why physiological alarms occur in intensive care units (ICUs). Instead of predicting disease or simply detecting abnormalities, Claris reconstructs the physiological context surrounding an alarm using ECG, arterial blood pressure (ABP), and pulse oximetry (SpO₂) signals to provide an evidence-based explanation of the event.

## Clinical Problem

ICU clinicians are exposed to thousands of physiological alarms every day, many of which are non-actionable or influenced by signal artifacts. This excessive alarm burden contributes to alarm fatigue and increases the risk of delayed responses to genuinely critical events.

## Research Objective

Develop an explainable playback framework that analyzes multimodal physiological signals surrounding an alarm and distinguishes physiologically supported events from artifact-driven or low-confidence alarms.
